# [02] Incident Handler's Journal - Ransomware Attack on Healthcare Clinic

**Scenario:** Small U.S. healthcare clinic hit by phishing email containing malicious attachment, leading to ransomware encryption of medical records. Business operations shut down on Tuesday 9:00 AM.

**Role:** Incident Handler / SOC Analyst - Documented the security incident.

**The 5 W's Analysis:**
- **Who:** Organized group of unethical hackers targeting healthcare
- **What:** Phishing email -> Malware -> Ransomware -> Encrypted files + Ransom note
- **When:** Tuesday morning ~9:00 AM
- **Where:** Healthcare clinic network and employee workstations
- **Why:** Financial gain via ransom, exploiting lack of security awareness

**Skills:** Incident Documentation, Phishing Analysis, Ransomware Response, 5 W's Framework
**Tools:** Incident Handler's Journal

**Key Learnings:** Importance of email security, backups, and incident reporting to CISA/FBI.
